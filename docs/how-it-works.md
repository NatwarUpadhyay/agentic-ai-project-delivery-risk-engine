# How It Works

## Step 1: Collect Inputs
The app collects three inputs from the user:
- Project Description
- Team Size
- Planned Timeline

## Step 2: Project Understanding
Agent 1 analyzes the scope, identifies complexity, stakeholders, and dependencies, and generates a project summary in business terms.

## Step 3: Risk Identification
Agent 2 produces a structured risk register spanning scope, schedule, resource, technology, security, compliance, and stakeholder categories.

## Step 4: Probability and Impact Scoring
Agent 3 assigns probability and business impact to each risk and provides a short justification for the scoring.

## Step 5: Mitigation Planning
Agent 4 generates mitigation actions, contingency plans, and preventive measures aligned to the identified risks.

## Step 6: Resource and Timeline Assessment
Agent 5 evaluates whether the proposed team size and timeline are realistic, identifies bottlenecks, and suggests adjustments.

## Step 7: Executive Reporting
Agent 6 consolidates all outputs into an executive report including:
- Executive summary
- Top risks
- Risk register table
- Project health score
- Success probability
- Recommendations and immediate actions

## Output Widgets
The app presents results as structured widgets for fast consumption:
- Project Summary
- Risk Register
- Risk Heat Map Summary
- Project Health Score
- Success Probability
- Mitigation Plan
- Executive Report
- Recommended Next Steps

## Tone and Style
The system uses a professional delivery and governance tone similar to a PMP-certified project manager, delivery manager, and AI consultant.
