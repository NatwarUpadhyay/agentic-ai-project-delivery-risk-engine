# Use Cases

## Pre-Kickoff Risk Assessment
Perform a structured risk assessment before project execution to surface scope, schedule, resource, and compliance risks early.

## RFP and Proposal Support
Help consulting and delivery teams quantify delivery risk and propose mitigation strategies during bid and proposal stages.

## Portfolio Health Review
Standardize risk scoring across multiple initiatives to prioritize staffing and investment decisions.

## Delivery Readiness Gate
Use the executive report as a go or no-go gate before mobilizing delivery teams and budgets.

## Regulated Industry Compliance Review
Assess security, data, and compliance risks for AI, data analytics, and digital transformation projects in regulated sectors.

## Client Executive Briefings
Generate an executive-ready summary with health score, success probability, and immediate action items.

## Resource and Timeline Planning
Evaluate the feasibility of proposed team size and timeline, and identify bottlenecks before execution.
